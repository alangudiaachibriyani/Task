Daily routine

How the system works

1
Every morning (7 AM) — Apps Script auto-generates today's daily log from templates and emails each person their tasks.
2
Staff open Daily Tracker — pick their name, see today's tasks with live countdown timers, mark done before cutoff.
3
30 min before cutoff — reminder email sent automatically if task is still Pending.
4
Works Tracker — admin creates one-off tasks anytime. Staff update status and comments from their view.
5
End of day (11 PM) — any unclosed daily tasks are automatically marked Missed with 0 score.
6
End of month — open Bonus Report, enter pool amount, get exact bonus figures per person based on combined scores.
